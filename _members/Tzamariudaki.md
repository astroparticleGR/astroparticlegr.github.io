Dr. Ekaterini Tzamariudaki — Director of Research in neutrino astronomy at INPP, NCSR "Demokritos", which she joined at the end of 2008. 
She holds a B.Sc. in Physics from AUTH and a Ph.D. in Particle Physics from McGill University, followed by positions as a DESY Fellow 
and Senior Researcher at the Max Planck Institute, Munich. There she worked on the H1 experiment at HERA, coordinating the SpaCal calorimeter group 
and convening the heavy flavour working group. Her recent research relates to the development of methods to distinguish the signal from cosmic neutrinos from the atmospheric muon background, 
to reliably differentiate muon track and shower events in the KM3NeT detector and to the analysis of the KM3NeT data as the construction of the detectors is progressing. 
Her research interests focus on the search for neutrinos from astrophysical sources. She also contributes to knowledge transfer, equality, diversity and inclusion, and several KM3NeT committees.
