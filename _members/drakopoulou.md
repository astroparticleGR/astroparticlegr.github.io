Dr. Evangelia Drakopoulou — Senior researcher in experimental astroparticle physics, specialising in neutrino experiments and advanced machine and deep learning techniques. She joined INPP at NCSR "Demokritos" in 2021, holding a B.Sc. (2009) and M.Sc. (2011) in Applied Mathematical and Physical Sciences from NTUA and a Ph.D. in Elementary Particle Physics (2016) on muon and neutrino energy reconstruction for KM3NeT. From 2016 to 2021 she was a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the University of Edinburgh, working on Hyper-Kamiokande, TITUS/E61 and ANNIE. She currently works on KM3NeT, ANNIE and Hyper-Kamiokande, developing ML techniques for rare-event detection, particle property prediction and data-driven analysis across scientific domains.
